# Share buybacks in the UK, Australia and New Zealand: research report

Prepared 5 November 2026. **Status: rules and market context only.** No company figures have been collected yet, so this report draws no conclusions about who is buying most. The app's Analysis section will show those once notices are saved. This is research, not investment advice, and the rules summary is not legal advice.

## What matters most

1. **Disclosure differs by market, and that decides the effort.** On ASX, one daily notice carries the running total. In the UK, notices vary and may now cover a whole week. On NZX, formats are mixed.
2. **UK reporting changed in 2026.** The FCA revised its rule on 27 February 2026, so weekly notices are now possible and fewer, larger UK notices may appear.
3. **The exchanges restrict automated collection.** A free, complete and automated tracker is not available from these sources without consent or a licence.

## How disclosure works

| | UK (London Stock Exchange) | Australia (ASX) | New Zealand (NZX) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Typical notice | "Transaction in Own Shares" | Appendix 3C "Notification of buy-back", with daily and final updates | "Notice of On-market Share Buyback"; some issuers use Appendix 3C |
| Timing | Formerly by 7.30am the next business day; [revised in February 2026](https://www.hsfkramer.com/notes/corporate/2026-posts/change-to-notification-requirements-on-a-share-buyback) to align with UK MAR, allowing one notice per week | Daily notice before trading on the next business day (listing rule 3.8A, as stated on the form) | Notices cite Listing Rule 3.13.1 and section 65(2) of the Companies Act 1993; timing not confirmed |
| Running total in the notice | Sometimes | Yes: before the previous day, and on the previous day | Varies |
| Price basis | Often pence, sometimes other currencies | Stated currency, per the form | Stated currency |
| Pricing or size limit | [UKLR 9.6](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/UKLR/9/6.html): 15% or more under a general authority must be a tender offer, unless shareholders approved the terms | Rule 7.33: a price cap of 5% above a recent average, as [quoted secondhand](https://strawman.com/forums/topic/7134). The form shows the cap each day. The 10/12 limit was not confirmed from a primary source | Not confirmed |

**To check before relying on this:** the current wording of UKLR 9.6.6R in the FCA Handbook (a cached page differed), the ASX Listing Rules text, and the New Zealand rules, for which no primary source was read.

## Singapore (added 10 November 2026)

Scope now includes SGX, for companies on a list to be supplied. This rests on three Daily Share Buy-Back Notices read on that date (Singtel, Hong Fok, Sarine), not on the SGX rulebook, which was not read.

- The notice is a fixed form: Section A the day's purchase, B off-market purchases, C cumulative shares since the mandate, D shares in issue and in treasury afterwards.
- Buying runs under a shareholder mandate with a maximum number of shares. A company may also name a programme with a money size, as Singtel does.
- A dual-listed company reports its overseas exchange in a second column, in that exchange's currency.
- SGX's websites are refused by the app, like the other exchanges' sites. Their terms were not reviewed.

## Hong Kong (added 15 November 2026)

Scope now includes HKEX, for companies on a list to be supplied. This rests on two daily Share Repurchase Reports and eight company returns read on that date, not on the HKEX rulebook, which was not read.

- **Three filings:** the daily Share Repurchase Report (every company that bought, an Excel file), the company's Next Day Disclosure Return (form FF305, the day after a change in its shares) and its Monthly Return (FF301). The return's Section II is the repurchase report: date, shares, method (on the Exchange or on another named exchange), highest and lowest price, amount paid, shares for cancellation and for treasury, and the repurchase mandate's date, size and amount used.
- **Several exchanges and currencies:** a Hong Kong company can buy in Hong Kong in HKD, in Shanghai or Shenzhen in RMB (A shares), in London in GBP and in New York in USD. Each purchase keeps its own exchange and currency; amounts are never added across currencies. A company such as HSBC, Standard Chartered or Prudential is also in the UK data, so a London purchase can be reported twice; the app marks it.
- **Cancelled or treasury:** stated on the return and in the report, but the report sometimes leaves one blank, which is shown as Not stated.
- HKEX's websites are refused by the app, like the other exchanges'. Their terms were not reviewed.

## Market context from secondary sources

These come from published articles, not from this tracker, and were not verified against notices.

- **Global:** Janus Henderson put [Q2 2026 buybacks at about US$572bn](https://www.investmentweek.co.uk/news/4535783/global-share-buybacks-jump-268-usd572bn-technology-overtakes-financials), up 26.8% on a year earlier, with technology ahead of financials. That is a scaled estimate; its 1,500-company index shows US$533.4bn.
- **UK:** AJ Bell reported [£31.1bn from FTSE 100 companies by April 2026](https://www.ajbell.co.uk/group/news/uk-share-buyback-bonanza-continues-despite-wider-uncertainties), behind the same stage of 2025. A [later note](https://www.ajbell.co.uk/news/why-ftse-100-investors-are-line-record-payout-2026) gave £36bn declared. These are amounts announced and are months old.
- **Australia and New Zealand:** no market-wide total was found. Individual notices seen include Brambles, Aurizon (up to A$250m), Karoon and Napier Port.

## Analyses the app runs

Each one is limited by the notices saved.

| Analysis | Question | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Markets | How many studied companies are buying, and how concentrated is it? | Notices |
| Pace | How much of each programme is used, against time passed? | A stated money size and dates |
| Sectors | Where is buying concentrated? | Notices; sector labels differ across markets |
| Share of issued capital | How much of the company was bought? | Shares on issue in a notice |
| Purchase prices | What did the company pay, first and latest? | Dated purchases |
| Announcement reaction | How did the share price move after an announcement? | Your own `inbox\prices.csv`; the result is not adjusted for the market and does not show cause |

Amounts are never added across currencies, so there is no combined three-market total.

## Limits of this work

- **Scope:** the study now follows companies you choose, by country and code, rather than a whole index, and shows each programme from start to latest notice rather than one calendar year. Sectors come from Wikipedia lists.
- **Reading accuracy:** tested on real ASX forms from 2025 and 2026, for New Zealand on Meridian Energy and Oceania Healthcare notices, and for the UK on HSBC notices. Other UK layouts are untested, so expect some to need review.
- **Coverage:** figures are only as complete as the notices you save. The app marks partial coverage.
